Motivation

Many riders depend on the MBTA subway to reach work, school, and other destinations. Delays can add time to a trip, but their effects may differ by line and change over time. Our dashboard helps riders and transportation stakeholders compare excess trip time across the Red, Orange, and Blue Lines and explore how ridership has changed alongside reliability.

Data Analysis

We use excess trip time data to compare observed trip times with baseline trip times. To prepare the data, we aggregate estimated passenger counts and weighted trip, travel, and wait-time components by subway line and month. We calculate passenger-weighted excess minutes by subtracting the summed baseline component from the summed observed component and dividing by the total estimated passengers for the selected period.
